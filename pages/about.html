About Divine CPA

Your trusted partner in financial success

Our Story

Divine CPA, PLLC was founded on the belief that exceptional accounting services should combine technical expertise with genuine personal attention. Located in Mesa, Arizona, we've built our practice on the foundation of helping individuals and businesses navigate the complex world of taxes and accounting with confidence.

Our approach is simple: we see beyond the numbers to understand your unique story, goals, and challenges. Whether you're a small business owner looking to optimize your operations, an individual seeking tax savings, or someone facing IRS issues, we're here to provide expert guidance with a personal touch.

What Sets Us Apart

We believe accounting is more than just number-crunching—it's about building relationships, understanding your vision, and helping you achieve your financial goals. Our team combines years of professional experience with a commitment to staying current on the latest tax laws and accounting practices.

When you work with Divine CPA, you're not just getting a tax preparer or bookkeeper—you're gaining a trusted advisor who's invested in your success. We take the time to understand your situation, explain complex concepts in plain language, and develop strategies tailored to your needs.
